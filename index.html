03 — How it works

Four moves. None of them lead back to you.

01

A wallet born here

anon makes a fresh keypair in your browser. It has never touched your main wallet, and its key never leaves this page.

02

Route SOL in

Your main wallet pays a one-time deposit address on NEAR Intents. A solver pays your anon wallet from its own funds. On chain, the trail stops there.

03

Trade any coin

Buy and sell any pump.fun coin from the anon wallet, on the curve or on PumpSwap. The anon wallet signs here; nothing else can.

04

Route out

Done? The anon wallet pays another route and your SOL lands back in your main wallet, from a solver's wallet, not from anon.

06 — What anon can't do

Unknown isn't invisible.

  • The route sees both ends. NEAR Intents' operator and the solver see the deposit and the payout. Nobody on chain links them; they could.
  • Timing and amounts can match. Route a round 1 SOL and buy a second later, and a patient watcher can guess. Odd amounts and a little waiting help.
  • Your browser holds the keys. Anon wallets live in this browser. Back them up. Clear your site data without a backup and they're gone.
  • Fresh wallets get flagged. Trackers mark new wallets as fresh. They can see that someone bought. They can't see who.
  • Memecoins are memecoins. A coin can go to zero whoever buys it.
